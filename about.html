Shyam Vinayak Films is a creative production partner based in Mumbai and Los Angeles, working with brands, founders and agencies across India and around the world.

We work across strategy, creative development and production, helping bring everything from campaign ideas and television commercials to digital films, branded content, corporate films and content ecosystems to life.

We carry years of producing independently, learning on real sets, with real budgets and real deadlines, what separates a film that performs from a film that just exists.

2024the year we formally came into existence, though the conversations started much earlier

When you're new to advertising, agencies and brands look at you differently. Agencies are protective of their clients, rightly so. When you're a newer production house, you don't walk in with a reputation.

You don't get the benefit of the doubt. You get the benefit of the work. So we'd rather earn it.

We've been handed briefs by people who had real reservations, and we've delivered. Some of those people now call us before the brief is even written. That's the only credibility that matters to us.

Over the years we've worked alongside exceptional agency teams, marketers and creative leaders.

Some of our proudest moments haven't come from awards or accolades. They've come from the emails that arrive after a project wraps:

  • The messages from agency teams appreciating the process.
  • The calls from creative directors who trusted us once and came back with another.
  • The recommendations that travel from one team to the next.

Still curious.

Still learning.

Still excited by a blank page.

And still convinced that the best work happens when strategy, creativity and execution sit at the same table. That's usually where you'll find us.
